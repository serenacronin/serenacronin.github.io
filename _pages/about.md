**A (theoretically) updated list of my publications: [NASA ADS](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/public-libraries/HI2y3dqzRXOKE1vWhZZ9vw).**

I am a PhD candidate at the University of Maryland, College Park. Broadly, I focus on multiwavelength observations of local galaxies to understand how processes in the interstellar medium, such as star formation and stellar feedback, impact the baryon cycle and galaxy evolution. I enjoy using a combination of methods including radio single-dish spectroscopy and interferometry, optical/IR integral field unit spectroscopy, and optical/UV/IR photometry. I have been awarded observing time with incredible telescopes such as JWST, ALMA and the VLA, and have also worked with data from VLT/MUSE, HST, GALEX, WISE, Spitzer, and Herschel. In addition to my thesis work, I have collaborated with [PHANGS](https://sites.google.com/view/phangs/the-phangs-team?authuser=0), [LGLBS](https://www.lglbs.org/team), and [EDGE](https://pages.astro.umd.edu/~bolatto/EDGE/).

Before my current position at UMD, I was a post-bac researcher at the National Radio Astronomy Observatory. I obtained my Bachelor of Science in Astronomy and Astrophysics at The Ohio State University in 2020. In my free time, I enjoy hanging out with my wife and dog, playing and watching basketball, reading books, weightlifting, and exploring interesting new places.
